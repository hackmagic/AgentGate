docs(skill): state which fields may never be dropped, and check it against output

§6.3 covered host frontmatter limits and what a reader must be able to *do*.
It did not cover what the renderer is forbidden to *omit* — and that gap was
real: the renderer drops `priority`, `escalation` and unphraseable condition
leaves, and nothing noticed.

Two requirements added, in both languages:

- trigger conditions must be visible: the event, every context entry, every
  condition branch, with no leaf removed;
- no field may be silently dropped, with a per-field table saying which must
  appear and which must not (`priority` is deliberately absent, because
  evaluation order is not the reader's business).

The check is a comparison against generated artifacts, not a reading of the
renderer, because a renderer can be correct about the fields it writes and
wrong about what it skips.

Worked verification is now a check rather than a paragraph:
`scripts/verify-skill-fields.ts` generates all five deep-work skills and
asserts 14 facts per policy — 5 policies, 0 failures, longest name 40 chars
against a limit of 64. Wired into `npm run check`.

Verified the check itself by removing `safetyLines()` from the renderer: it
reported `audit_level` missing on all five policies and `requires_approval` on
the two that ask for approval. Restored, clean.
